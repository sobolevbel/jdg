# Registering as a VAT-UE taxpayer

!!! question "Why register for VAT-UE"
    If you plan to provide services to companies in other EU countries, you generally need to register for VAT-UE before starting.

!!! note
    By this point you should already have NIP and REGON. See [the VAT-UE registration requirements][1].

## 0. Getting started

On [Biznes.gov.pl][2], choose **Zmień dane w CEIDG** under **Usługi CEIDG**, then click **Rozpocznij**.

If **Podpowiadanie danych** appears, select your profile and click **Dalej**.

## 1. Important information first

Read it, then hit `DALEJ`

![Важная информация перед изменением CEIDG, кнопка Dalej][3]

## 2. Purpose of filing the form

Choose **Chcę edytować wpis, wysłać dokumenty do ZUS lub urzędu skarbowego** and enter **Data powstania zmiany**.

This is the CEIDG change date, not the VAT registration date.

![Цель изменения CEIDG и поле Data powstania zmiany][4]

## 3. Pick what you want to change

Under **Załączniki**, select **Podatek VAT: VAT-R**. The wizard also requires social insurance, the tax office and e-Doręczenia; it may select related address and contact fields automatically.

![Раздел Załączniki: отмечено приложение Podatek VAT: VAT-R][5]

If you are a non-EU citizen, follow the instruction on this page to select **Dane osobowe, obywatelstwo** and provide the current residence document details.

![Обязательные разделы CEIDG и выбор Dane osobowe, obywatelstwo][6]

Leave unrelated changes, including PIT and additional ZUS forms, unselected.

## 4. Foreigner's data

Enter the document that gives you the right to run a JDG in Poland and its issuing authority.

![Данные документа иностранца и выбор органа выдачи][7]

See [the JDG registration guide][8] for the document fields.

## 5. Residential address

Change your residential address if needed.

![Адрес проживания: выбор адреса и кнопка Dodaj nowy adres][9]

### Contacts and e-Doręczenia

The wizard may also ask for company contacts. Publication consent is separate for each contact; choose **Nie** if you do not want the supplied details published in CEIDG.

![Контакты фирмы и отдельное согласие на публикацию e-mail][10]

If you already have an e-Doręczenia address, the wizard displays it and the owner's details. Check them and click **Dalej**.

![Существующий адрес e-Doręczenia и кнопка Dalej][11]

On **Administratorzy skrzynki e-Doręczeń**, leave the checkboxes empty if you are not changing administrators.

![Администраторы e-Doręczenia: без изменений, кнопка Dalej][12]

If you do not yet have an address, complete the creation or registration steps shown by the wizard.

## 6. Social insurance

Select your current insurance institution, normally **ZUS**.

![Социальное страхование: выбран ZUS][13]

## 7. Changing ZUS data

If you are not changing your insurance, leave the forms under **Zgłoszenia do ZUS** unselected.

## 8. Tax office

You can switch tax offices if you need to.

![Выбор налоговой инспекции перед заполнением VAT-R][14]

## 9. The actual point of the application

Choose **Rejestracja podatnika VAT** if you have not previously registered as an active or exempt VAT taxpayer. If Polish VAT or VAT-UE is already registered, choose **Aktualizacja danych podatnika VAT**.

![Цель VAT-R: выбран вариант Aktualizacja danych podatnika VAT][15]

## 10. Domestic Polish VAT

Choose whether you will use the Polish VAT exemption.

- If you do not need Polish VAT, choose **Tak**, then **art. 113 ust. 1 lub 9 ustawy**. Enter the exemption start date.
- If you already have active Polish VAT registration, choose **Nie**.
- If you want to register for active Polish VAT at the same time, use [the Polish VAT registration instructions][16].

![Освобождение от польского VAT: Tak, art. 113 и дата начала освобождения][17]

The **[art. 113a][18]** option is for businesses established in another EU country.

## 11. Identification data

Father's and mother's names. Best if the Latin spelling matches what's in their documents. Just in case.

![Идентификационные данные VAT-R: имена родителей][19]

### If you already have active Polish VAT registration

On **Aktualizacja danych podatnika VAT**, answer **Nie** to the tax-office change question if the office stays the same.

![Актуализация VAT-R без смены налогового органа][20]

On **Obowiązek podatkowy w zakresie podatku od towarów i usług (VAT)**:

1. Under **Wybierz tytuł podatkowy**, choose the same reason as in your original Polish VAT registration.
2. Under **Data rozpoczęcia tytułu obowiązku podatkowego**, enter the same date. Check your old VAT-R or ask your accountant.

![Прежнее основание польского VAT и дата начала налоговой обязанности][21]

!!! warning "Leave financial services unselected"
    Leave both options under **Zwolnienie usług finansowych** unselected.

![Блок Zwolnienie usług finansowych: оба варианта не отмечены][22]

For the cash method, special procedures and quarterly returns, choose **Bez zmian** if you are keeping the existing settings.

![Актуализация VAT-R: Bez zmian для метода, процедур и квартальных деклараций][23]

## 12. VAT-UE registration

Choose **Chcę zarejestrować się do VAT-UE**. If you are updating VAT-R, an **Jestem zarejestrowanym podatnikiem VAT UE** option may also appear; use it only if VAT-UE is already registered.

![VAT-UE: вариант Chcę zarejestrować się do VAT-UE][24]

## 13. Registering as a VAT UE taxpayer

This screen depends on your Polish VAT status.

**If you retain Polish VAT exemption**, select the applicable **Tytuły obowiązku rejestracji**:

- for services, use the option mentioning sales of services to EU taxpayers for which the customer accounts for VAT, or imports of services;
- select the intra-Community acquisition of goods option only if it applies to your transactions.

![VAT-UE при освобождении от польского VAT: отмечены услуги, указана дата][25]

**If you are already an active Polish VAT taxpayer**, the screen instead shows an explanation of EU transactions and a date field.

![VAT-UE для активного плательщика польского VAT: поле даты операций][26]

Under **Przewidywana data rozpoczęcia transakcji wewnątrzwspólnotowych**, enter the planned start date of EU transactions, not your original Polish VAT registration date.

### VAT returns for an existing Polish VAT taxpayer

If **Rodzaj składanych deklaracji VAT** appears, keep the existing return type and first Polish VAT return period. Adding VAT-UE does not change them.

![Тип польской декларации VAT и первый отчётный период][27]

For the exempt services-only route above, this screen is skipped.

## 14. Applicant information

You can pick another tax office and add contact details if you want. Optional — but it can make talking to the office easier.

![Налоговая и необязательные контактные данные в VAT-R][28]

## 15. Confirmation of VAT taxpayer registration

You probably don't need this.

![Отказ от платного подтверждения регистрации VAT][29]

## 16. Liability warning

On your own head be it — you confirm you want to file and you're not up to anything shady.

![Oświadczenie: заявление о праве на адреса и отсутствии запретов][30]

## 17. Where to get more info

Just hit `DALEJ`

## 18. Summary screen

Just hit `DALEJ`

## 19. Signing documents

!!! tip
    Click the xml links to download the forms for your records.

Pick how you sign. Trusted profile and bank login are basically the same thing. If you set up trusted profile through a bank, you can use bank login.

## 20. Sending documents

After a quick sign-and-send, you're done.

## 21. Speeding up VAT-UE registration

The tax office may ask for documents confirming your right to use the business address, for example:

"w związku ze złożeniem do tutejszego Urzędu Skarbowego złożenia druku rejestracyjnego VAT-R, proszę o przestawienie prawa do lokalu w którym jest wykonywana działalność gospodarcza."

Reply with documents appropriate to your situation, such as a lease or the property owner's consent. See [the VAT registration guide][31] for an example.

!!! tip
    This step isn't mandatory, but it can shave a bit of time off the process.
    Same idea as for [domestic VAT][31] registration.

## 22. Wrap-up

Wait for confirmation that CEIDG accepted the application, or a request to correct it. The tax office may separately ask for supporting documents.

Check VAT-UE registration in [VIES][32]. Your VAT-UE number is your NIP prefixed with **PL**. Acceptance of the CEIDG application is not itself confirmation that the number is already active in VIES.

There is also a [Telegram bot][33] for monitoring the registration status.
